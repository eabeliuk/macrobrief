# MacroBrief — Art Direction

**Referent:** the wire desk's daily **news budget** — the lineup sheet an editor keeps of every
story in play, one ruled row each: slug in caps, the headline, who has it, how many desks moved
it, and the decision (RUNS / HELD). It is the document where "what reaches the reader" is actually
decided, which is the product's whole claim and the mark's whole meaning (the world behind, the
M in front choosing).

**Grid:** the sheet's. A ruled table with a fixed slug gutter in monospace caps, headline column
that takes the slack, figures right-aligned, a decision column at the far edge. Above the sheet,
an asymmetric 2:5 split held for the rest of the page: a narrow label column on the left, content
on the right. No cards.

**Type:** display and text in **Open Sans** (the family face — the wordmark is set in it, so the
page and the lockup read as one thing). Slugs, datelines, timings and figures in **IBM Plex
Mono** — the wire convention: `CHILE-LITHIUM/ROYALTY (UPDATE 2)`, `17 SEP 05:12 UTC`. Figures
right-aligned, tabular numerals.

**Palette:** `brandbook/tokens.css` — ink `#14161f`, signal `#1d4ed8` (the briefing genre's trust
blue), paper `#f4f5f8`, paper-dark `#0f1117`. On the sheet, one more: a hairline rule derived from
paper. The signal is used for decisions and links only, never for surfaces.

**Ornament:** rules. Hairline row rules, a heavier rule under the sheet's header, a double rule
to close it. Nothing else.

**The risk:** the hero **is** the sheet, and the sheet is **live**: today's real budget for three
showcase topics the platform itself follows, ranked by the product's own editor (mentions ×
publisher tier, duplicates collapsed) minutes before the page was served. Behind it, a
**computed orthographic globe** — graticule projected in code, the back hemisphere culled, and a
dateline pin for every publisher on today's sheet whose desk we can place. Every visit is a
different sheet. Nothing on the page is a mockup.

**Never:**
- Feature cards, three-column grids, `01 / 02 / 03` steps, an FAQ.
- Invented headlines. If the showcase has no items yet, the sheet says so in the sheet's own
  voice ("NO BUDGET — first poll pending") rather than showing a placeholder story.
- The signal colour as a background. It is the decision ink, not paint.
- A newspaper-front-page pastiche: no masthead rules, drop caps, columns of body type, or
  "Extra! Extra!" voice. The referent is the *internal* sheet, not the printed product.

**Technique registered:** computed orthographic globe (graticule + dateline pins, hidden
hemisphere culled) + live budget sheet from the product's own ranker.
