The mark

MacroBrief inherits the family’s isometric M on the family canvas 0 0 1000 698.86, and seats its object in the M’s notch on the same band the sibling marks put their rhombus tiles on — but, unlike them, it draws the object behind the M.

What sits in the notch is what differs, and it is the only part that carries meaning.

BrandIn the notchSays
MacroPrimerthree tiles, scattereddispersed knowledge
MacroAccountfour tiles, one blockaccounts resolving into one position
MacroKBone tile, cut in strataone body of knowledge in layers
MacroBriefa globe, rising behind the Mthe world behind; the M selects what reaches you

The globe is the news icon everyone already reads, drawn the way those icons are: a disc with its equator and one meridian knocked out, plus the horizon. It is large — the mark’s subject, not an ornament — and seated low, so the M’s V cuts its lower third and the whole bottom arc is hidden. The world is behind; the M, in front, is the thing that decides what gets through.

Construction

Geometry is derived, not drawn. Every asset in this book is generated from these constants by brandbook/build.py.

The full circle, dashed, and the M it hides behind

globe     centre (500, 290) · r 275
graticule  equator rx 275 · ry 104.5  (foreshorten 0.38)
           meridian rx 104.5 · ry 275
           horizon y = 290 · knock-out 18
gap       the M’s outline, knocked out at 18, globe side

The centre and radius are chosen together: the top clears y = 0, and at every x the circle’s lower arc lies below the M’s inner edge, so nothing of the bottom shows through the notch. Move one and the other must move with it.

§2 Construction3

The graticule and the gap

The equator, meridian and horizon are knocked out as transparent lines (width 18) in every variant, not only the single-ink ones. Filled solid, the globe is a plain disc — a coin, a period — and the idea is gone.

The same knock-out runs along the M’s top edge, one seam wide, on the globe’s side. Without it the one-ink variants fuse globe and M into a single blob, and the “behind” reading — which is the mark — is lost.

Why fuchsia

An alerts product needs an accent that says look here and still cannot be mistaken for an error: red is the one colour every interface already spends on failure, and it is MacroMkt’s besides. Fuchsia has the urgency without the meaning.

It is also unclaimed across the family: MacroBP gold, MacroReply violet, MacroPrimer green, MacroSkill indigo, MacroAccount blue, MacroKB lime, MacroLinker cyan.

§5 Type6

Clear space, size, and never

Clear space

Half the globe’s radius on all sides — 137.5 units, about 14% of the mark’s width. Nothing sets inside it.

Minimum size

64 px
32 px
24 px — the floor

Below about 20 px the graticule closes and the globe becomes a disc. Use favicon.svg at 16 px — it is centred on the mark’s true bounding box precisely because a 16 px tab cannot spare the height.

Never

  • Stretch the mark to fill a square. It is wider than tall; letterbox it.
  • Fill the graticule. Solid, the globe is a coin.
  • Put the globe in front of the M, or lift it clear of it. Behind is the point.
  • Add a dot, pin or badge to the globe. The M already does the selecting.
  • Redraw the M. It is shared with every Macro brand and is not ours to change.
  • Hand-edit the SVGs. Change the constants and re-run build.py.
